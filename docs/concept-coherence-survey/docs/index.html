BEFORE YOU START

A few things to know.

Describe visible patterns

A shared pattern might involve color, texture, lines, edges, shapes, objects, or their arrangement. Different objects can share the same visual pattern.

Make your own judgment

Groups may be strongly related, weakly related, or unrelated. Do not assume every patch belongs. Use “No clear shared pattern” when appropriate.

Work independently

Do not discuss responses with other participants. Base your answers on visible evidence; do not infer movement from a still image.

One group at a time

You will describe the pattern first, then rate clarity and identify outliers. Submitted groups are locked. Drafts are saved automatically; sign in with the same email to resume.

Timing starts when the first group opens. You may take breaks; active tab time and total elapsed time are recorded separately.
